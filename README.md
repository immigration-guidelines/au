# Immigration guidance: Australia

Published guidance that the authorities of Australia issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** none yet. The Department of Home Affairs' instructions to its decision-makers (the Procedural Instructions, formerly the Procedures Advice Manual) are not freely published: they are available through LEGENDcom, the department's subscription database, which is behind a paywall. **Contributions are welcome.** If you have lawful access to instructions that may be shared, or copies released under freedom of information (for example from the department's FOI disclosure log), please open a pull request adding them to a `guidance/` folder, with the date of each document and where it came from.
- **Country-of-origin reports:** 23 files in `coi/`, with their own `coi/index.tsv`. These are the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade's country information reports, dated July 2023 to September 2026 (one, Colombia, undated in its file name). They were added on 15 November 2026; `coi/index.tsv` gives the source address of each.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
